Shift lists are entered like this:

56.2;.2
64.1;.6

The first column (56.2/64.1) is the X value of the shift. After the ; an intensity can be given and a multiplicity directly appended to it. Intensities and multiplicities are optional. Intensities can be in any range, they will be auto-scaled to the 0-1 range.
In QuickCheck diastereotopic pairs can be entered in adjacent lines, with a + in front of the second value:
1.8
+1.6

You can also do a 13C/1H correlation search by chosing the option "13C/1H correlation" and entering a shift table like this:
56.2;2.2
64.1;2.6
This searches for molecules where 56.2 and 2.2 are on neighbouring carbon/hydrogen atoms, as well as 64.1 and 2.6.
